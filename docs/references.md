# References and links
In this file, a collection of links and references can be found.

* Documentation of LightningCast. Relevant channels are C02 (cloud optical thickness), C05 (cloud phase), C13 (Surface or cloud-top temperature), C15 (Cloud-top temperature/height).  
  https://cimss.ssec.wisc.edu/probsevere/wp-content/uploads/sites/29/2025/11/LightningCast_QuickGuide.pdf
* MTG FCI level 1c data guide. Relevant channels are VIS 0.6 (identical), NIR 1.6 (identical), IR 10.5 (deviation: 10.5µm instead of 10.35µm of C13), IR 12.3 (identical).  
  https://user.eumetsat.int/resources/user-guides/mtg-fci-level-1c-data-guide
* Documentation of the GOES satellites ABI. Page 3 for channels and corresponding wavelengths.  
  https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2021-08/GOES-16_ABI-L1b-CMI_Full-Validation_ProductPerformanceGuide_v2.pdf
  https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2024-02/G18_ABI_L1b-CMI_FullValidation_ProductPerformanceGuide_Publish.pdf
